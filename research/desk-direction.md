**Idea.** On this page, Ethan's own desk at night in Nashville (the AT&T Building's twin spires in the window, a chessboard, a frog, a Vanderbilt mug) becomes the explorable index of his work: each flagship project is a physical object on it, and scrolling flies the camera to each one.
